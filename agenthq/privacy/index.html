AgentHQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

AgentHQ is a private personal application.

The application uses Google OAuth to access Google Drive data authorized by its user. This data is used only to provide the application's functionality.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or made available to other users.

Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings.

AgentHQ's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
